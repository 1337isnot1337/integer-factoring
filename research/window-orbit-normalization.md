# Normalize Fourier windows on the target cycle

The [registered integration question](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/integer-factoring/discussions/51#discussioncomment-19339245)
refines the [window-duality checkpoint](window-lemma-character-duality.md).
For the actual factoring walk, all starts lie in the cycle generated by
the target prime form. Characters trivial on that cycle contribute to
its positive mean; counting them as absolute error discards this fact.

The result below is an exact finite-group identity and sufficient window
criterion. It does not prove an affordable uniform arithmetic window,
equate one major-arc bound with equidistribution or improve the complete
factoring score. The score remains **1/2**.

## 1. A positive autocorrelation and its restricted spectrum

Let H be a finite abelian group, A a nonempty inversion-symmetric subset,
g in H of order T and K=⟨g⟩. Use additive notation for A-A and
multiplicative notation for the cycle. Set

~~~
f(x)=#{(a,b) in A²: a-b=x}/|A|,
sigma(chi)=sum_(a in A) chi(a)/|A|.
~~~

Then 0<=f<=1, its support is A-A, and its H-Fourier coefficient is
`(|A|/|H|)*|sigma(chi)|²`. For phases theta in `(1/T)Z/Z`, aggregate

~~~
C_theta=sum_(chi(g)=exp(2pi i theta)) |sigma(chi)|².
~~~

Every C_theta is real and nonnegative. Fourier inversion restricted to
K gives the exact identity

~~~
f(g^j)=(|A|/|H|)*sum_theta C_theta exp(2pi i j theta).       (1)
~~~

In particular the mean on the target cycle is
`mu_K=(|A|/|H|)*C_0`, with C_0>0. Positivity follows also from
`f(1)=1` and the presence of the identity in K.

Let r_Q count the elements of A in each coset Q of K. Character
orthogonality on H/K yields an exact acquisition formula:

~~~
C_0=[H:K]*sum_Q r_Q²/|A|².                              (2)
~~~

Cauchy--Schwarz gives C_0>=1. If A lies in one coset then
C_0=[H:K]. This is the orbit mean multiplier, not a count of only the
global principal character.

For the arithmetic application, A=phi(B_a), with injectivity identifying
the image cardinality with |B_a|. If 2a<=R, the norm triangle inequality
in the corrected split/ramified quotient gives A-A⊆S. A positive f
window therefore supplies a smooth class. No uniform estimate is inferred
merely from this inclusion.

## 2. A correctly normalized sufficient condition

For an integer W>=1 define

~~~
E(W)=sum_(theta!=0) C_theta
                   min(1,1/(2W*||theta||)).
~~~

The finite geometric-sum bound and (1) imply, for every integer start L,

~~~
sum_(j=0)^(W-1) f(g^(L+j))
  >= (|A|/|H|)*W*(C_0-E(W)).                            (3)
~~~

Thus **E(W)<C_0** suffices for every W-step window on K to meet A-A,
and hence S in the stated arithmetic setting. It is a sufficient
condition, not a necessary one: oscillating contributions can cancel
more effectively than the absolute bound. The actual least-power start
is included, but no estimate for its required W has been proved here.

The condition is independent of enlarging the ambient group. Every
character of H has [H':H] extensions to a larger finite abelian H'.
When A and g stay in H, all C_theta, including C_0, are multiplied by
the same factor; E(W)/C_0 is unchanged. This separates exact ambient
class-group generation from the intrinsic target-cycle estimate.

## 3. All scales of nonzero phase mass enter the bound

Put

~~~
M(u)=sum_(0<||theta||<=u) C_theta,        0<u<=1/2.
~~~

The exact layer-cake identity is

~~~
E(W)=M(1/2)/W
     +(1/(2W))*integral_(1/(2W))^(1/2) M(u)/u² du.       (4)
~~~

For each phase distance d, integrate from max(d,1/(2W)) to1/2.
Its integral contribution plus C_theta/W is exactly
`C_theta*min(1,1/(2Wd))`; summing proves (4). Boundary atoms do not
affect the integral. A bound on M near1/W alone does not control the
remaining integral. A quantitative arithmetic estimate must either
bound the complete expression, supply adequate mass bounds at every
relevant scale, or retain additional signed cancellation.

The global S-density need not equal the density on a particular cycle.
Equation (2) keeps that distinction explicit. No replacement of the
arithmetic problem by a density-only hitting argument is made.

## 4. A small example where normalization is essential

Take `H=(Z/4)²`, `A={(0,0),(1,0),(3,0)}` and `g=(1,0)`. Then

~~~
C_0=4,       C_(1/4)=C_(1/2)=C_(3/4)=4/9.
~~~

For W=1, E(W)=4/3<4, so (3) certifies every one-step window on the
target cycle. Directly, f on that cycle has values `1,2/3,2/3,2/3`.
Every element in the coset with second coordinate one has f=0, so
uniform windows over all of H fail for arbitrarily long lengths.
The group-principal absolute-error criterion has a permanent error
of at least C_0-1=3, and cannot prove this one-step target-cycle fact.
This is a distinction of domains, not a stronger factoring window.

## 5. Exact scope controls and open quantitative step

Run `make verify-window-orbit-duality`. The
[checker](../tools/check_window_orbit_duality.py) uses only rational
arithmetic on ten prescribed group/target cases of exponents2,3,4,6.
Inversion symmetry makes their Fourier coefficients rational, so no
floating root-of-unity comparisons enter. The
[receipt](../evidence/window-orbit-duality-controls.json) records37
restriction values,74 exact step-function integrals and69 strictly
certified finite windows, along with the quotient-coset collision formula.
These are controls for the general proof, not arithmetic experiments.

It also checks the actual discriminant -20 counterexample to the
unquotiented ramified relation claim, and finite controls for the
conditional pigeonhole phase lemma. The imported saved-data analysis
replay is recorded separately; no fresh PARI class-group scan is claimed.

What remains open is a uniform bound on E(W)/C_0 for the specific
weighted prime-form balls, at W affordable under the complete factoring
invoice. The formulas identify what to estimate; they do not supply that
bound. Further analytic work must use the arithmetic/coset structure,
rather than promote the finite group controls or one-radius mass statement.
