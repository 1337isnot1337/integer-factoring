# The transposition class sum and replica swap are not enough at ten sites

## Result

For the coherent bounded-spin crossing, let `R` be the replica swap on the
row cut and let `T` be the compressed diagonal transposition class sum

```math
T=\sum_{i<j}T_{ij}\otimes T_{ij}
```

from the [second-coefficient identity](bounded-spin-second-coefficient.md).
At length eight, `R` and `T` generate the full six-dimensional row algebra
and reproduce its five primary dimensions

```text
20, 1, 43, 70, 35.
```

At length ten they generate only a proper six-dimensional subalgebra. Its
five joint generalized eigenspaces have dimensions

```text
35, 155, 225, 335, 406.
```

The published full eight-dimensional ten-site row algebra refines the coarse
`335` block as

```math
335=34+1+300.
```

Thus the second coefficient and the replica swap do not generate the ten-site
coefficient algebra. A twelve-site primary-decomposition calculation that uses
only `R` and `T` would see a coarsening, not the full central decomposition.
No factoring exponent changes.

## The two computations

Use the normalized path frame and the compression
`(T_{ij})_{ab}=<v_a,P_{ij}v_b>/nu_b` over `F_5`. Build

```math
T=\sum_{i<j}T_{ij}\otimes T_{ij},\qquad
R(a,b)=(b,a).
```

The two operators commute and satisfy `R^2=1`. The primary dimensions below
are the dimensions of the joint generalized eigenspaces of `(T,R)`, computed
by exact rank over `F_5`. The algebra dimension is the dimension of the
algebra generated by `T` and `R`.

| Length | `q` | algebra dimension | `(T,R)` generalized dimensions |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 8 | 13 | 6 | `(2,+):70`, `(3,+):1`, `(3,-):43`, `(4,+):20`, `(4,-):35` |
| 10 | 34 | 6 | `(0,+):35`, `(0,-):406`, `(1,+):225`, `(2,+):335`, `(2,-):155` |

At eight sites the five dimensions are exactly the published row primary
dimensions. At ten sites the five coarse dimensions sum to `34^2=1156`, but
they merge three published primary blocks. The full ten-site row algebra has
primary dimensions

```text
34, 1, 225, 300, 35, 406, 155
```

from the [ten-site algebra certificate](bounded-spin-correction-modules.md).
The only discrepancy is `335=34+1+300`; the other four coarse dimensions
`35,155,225,406` are already primary.

## Consequence for the twelve-site target

The image-floor obstruction closed the single-low-rank correction route, but
left open the full central decomposition at twelve sites. The present
computation shows that the first two structural generators are insufficient
there as well: the twelve-site primary decomposition must include at least one
further coefficient direction that splits the `R,T`-coarse blocks. The
`R,T` coarse dimensions are therefore a diagnostic partition, not a rank
certificate.

This also explains why the eight-site success of the second-coefficient
identity does not extend automatically: the identity identifies the whole
six-dimensional algebra at eight sites, but only a codimension-two subalgebra
at ten sites. A uniform twelve-site calculation must add the missing
directions before evaluating the two-cut rank pairs.
