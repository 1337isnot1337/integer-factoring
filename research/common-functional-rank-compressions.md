# Common-functional rank compressions without coefficient algebras

A direct subspace certificate closes the complete eight-direction
coefficient space of the 80-label, five-site, two-even-site construction
over every field of characteristic two and at every tensor power.
The certificate gives constant scalar compressions of dimensions
**2,072 on the row cut and 286 on the column cut** for the same linear
functional. Their product is

\[
2072\cdot286=592592>80^3=512000.
\tag{1}
\]

Consequently every crossing decomposition in the tensor-power span has
cost at least \(592592^{n/2}>80^{3n/2}>80^{\sqrt2 n}\).
This extends to mixed products with the previously certified 24- and
26-label spaces. It does not exclude other input or output maps.

The reusable method searches for a subspace that is disjoint from all of
its non-scalar images. It does not compute the algebra generated by the
coefficient matrices or require that the functional be an algebra
character.

## 1. The subspace criterion

Let \(M_0=I,M_1,\ldots,M_{s-1}\) be matrices over a field \(k\), and
fix scalars \(\chi_0=1,\chi_1,\ldots,\chi_{s-1}\). For a subspace
\(U\), put

\[
Z(U)=\sum_{i=0}^{s-1}(M_i-\chi_iI)U.
\]

**Lemma.** If

\[
U\cap Z(U)=\{0\},
\tag{2}
\]

and \(b=\dim U\), there are constant matrices \(V\) and \(W\) such
that

\[
WV=I_b,\qquad WM_iV=\chi_i I_b\quad(0\le i<s).
\tag{3}
\]

**Proof.** Take the columns of \(V\) to be a basis of \(U\). On the
direct sum \(U\oplus Z(U)\), define \(W\) as the coordinate map of
that basis on \(U\) and zero on \(Z(U)\). Extend it linearly to the
ambient space. Then \(W(M_i-\chi_iI)V=0\), proving (3).

Condition (2) has a simple finite certificate: the proposed vectors of
\(U\), a basis of their difference-image span, and full rank of their
concatenation. The checker can also solve explicitly for \(W\) and
test every entry of (3). No structural hypothesis on the generated
matrix algebra is needed.

## 2. Use the same functional on both cuts

Let \(T_0=E,T_1,\ldots,T_{s-1}\) be an independent tensor basis whose
crossing \(E\) becomes the identity in both flattenings. Define
\(\chi(T_i)=\chi_i\). Suppose (3) holds in the row cut with size
\(a\), and in the column cut with size \(b\).
For every tensor \(T\) in the coefficient span,

\[
W_r M_r(T)V_r=\chi(T)I_a,\qquad
W_c M_c(T)V_c=\chi(T)I_b.
\tag{4}
\]

Thus \(\chi(T)\ne0\) forces both \(r(T)\ge a\) and \(c(T)\ge b\).
The functional need not be multiplicative on either coefficient
algebra; linearity on the tensor span is sufficient.

Tensoring (4) proves, for arbitrary
\(T\in\operatorname{span}\{T_i\}^{\otimes n}\),

\[
\chi^{\otimes n}(T)\ne0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
r(T)\ge a^n,\qquad c(T)\ge b^n.
\tag{5}
\]

The pieces need not be pure tensors. For a decomposition
\(E^{\otimes n}=\sum_j H_j\) in this space,
\(\sum_j\chi^{\otimes n}(H_j)=1\). At least one summand has nonzero
functional, so

\[
\sum_j\sqrt{r(H_j)c(H_j)}\ge(ab)^{n/2}.
\tag{6}
\]

When all matrices and witnesses are binary, (3) remains valid under
every characteristic-two field extension. Equations (5)--(6) then
hold over every such field, including infinite ones.

## 3. The five-site code

Use five physical mode pairs. A logical label specifies two even sites
\(a<b\) and one single-mode orientation at each of the other three
sites. Its input vector is

\[
\left(\text{product of the three single modes}\right)(D_a+D_b),
\]

and its output selects the term containing \(D_b\).
There are \(\binom52 2^3=80\) labels, all of physical exterior grade
five. This is the restriction of `boundary_code(5,5)` to syndrome
weight three. The literal contraction verifies the crossing endpoint,
the zero endpoint at \(p\), and cut ranks \((1,6400)\) at \(q\).

Its complete scalar polynomial coefficient span has dimension eight.
In the independent raw basis used by the literal evaluator, the
corresponding leading monomials are

\[
r^5,\;pr^4,\;p^2r^3,\;p^3r^2,\;p^4r,\;
qr^4,\;pqr^3,\;p^2qr^2.
\]

Choose

\[
\chi=(1,1,1,1,0,0,0,0).
\tag{7}
\]

Applying this functional to the full polynomial family gives exactly

\[
\chi(G(p,q,r))=(q+r)^2(p+r)^3.
\tag{8}
\]

The checker verifies (8) as a formal binary polynomial identity, not
only at field points. At the crossing, \((p,q,r)=(0,0,1)\), its value
is one.

Both cuts split into 16 syndrome blocks: one of size 640 and fifteen
of size 384. The direct subspace criterion yields the following

comparison \(3/2>\sqrt2\) follows from \(9>8\); no numerical threshold
is required.

## 4. Mixed products with the 24- and 26-label spaces

The [earlier common-character theorem](symplectic-seed-character-barrier.md)
gives row triangular restrictions of dimensions 384 and 436 and
column scalar compressions of dimension 43 for the 24- and 26-label
spaces. A scalar identity is also triangular. Therefore tensoring
those certificates with (4) yields, for any nonempty mixed product
containing \(n\) copies of the 24-label space, \(m\) copies of the
26-label space, and \(h\) copies of the 80-label space,

\[
S\ge16512^{n/2}18748^{m/2}592592^{h/2}
>(24^n26^m80^h)^{3/2}.
\tag{9}
\]

This holds for the full mixed tensor-product span and arbitrary
linear recombinations over every characteristic-two field. Common
or independent scalar parameter curves cannot evade the result,
because all their coefficients remain in that span.

Changing the code, adding tensor directions outside these spans, or
changing the output maps creates a new problem. This theorem supplies
no lower bound for those alternatives or for integer factoring.

## 5. Search and independent verification

The discovery procedure starts with \(U=0\). It tries shuffled
coordinate vectors followed by reproducible pseudorandom vectors.
A vector is accepted precisely when the enlarged subspace still
satisfies (2). It uses high-pivot binary elimination and three fixed
restarts, retaining the largest certificate separately in each
syndrome block. The resulting dimensions need not be optimal.

The independent checker uses low-pivot elimination and explicitly
constructs all left maps \(W\). It verifies their action on every
difference image, verifies \(WV=I\), and then checks every entry of
\(WM_iV=\chi_iI\) for all eight generators. It checks 2,392,944 row-cut
entries and 48,928 column-cut entries. This verifies the claimed
constant maps directly; it does not infer them from the search log.

The default checker regenerates the literal coefficient matrices from
the code, including 2,621,440 contracted entries and 10,485,760
individual input-support contributions. A deterministic digest binds
those matrices to the witness certificate. A precomputed basis can
be supplied for faster replay, but it must have the same digest and
logical inputs and outputs.

```sh
python tools/check_common_character_compressions.py \
  --output /tmp/symplectic-two-even-P5-compression.json
```
