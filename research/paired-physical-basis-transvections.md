# Paired physical changes of basis over F4

## Result and scope

This checkpoint tests elementary changes of the five physical labels of
the retained degree-18 crossing. The changes preserve the complete
crossing endpoint and polynomial degree. They act on the physical tensor
before either weighted Smith calculation.

The completed finite screen contains 80 single-move coefficient orbits
and 2,000 pairs with one move on each paired boundary. It records no
improvement over the reference at either fixed gauge. The reference
costs remain approximately `0.7263479477044683` and
`0.8335422390094076`. This does not exclude other physical bases, longer
words, other gauges, or other scales. Numerical scale minimization is
exploratory.

This family differs from the
[equal-order Smith-row flags](f4-equal-order-smith-flags.md) and
[polynomial Smith-row flags](f4-polynomial-smith-flags.md). Those flags
change constraints used to synthesize a tensor. The maps here change
the physical tensor itself, with the inverse map on its matching label.
They also differ from
[complementary Gaussian columns](aligned-gaussian-complement-perturbations.md),
which add vectors from omitted physical states.

## 1. Exact paired transformation

Let the endpoint of a polynomial tensor be

\[
T_{xyuv}(1)=[x=v][y=u].
\]

For an invertible constant matrix \(S\), apply \(S\) on the \(X\)
index and \(S^{-T}\) on the \(V\) index. Then

\[
T'_{xyuv}(1)
=\sum_{a,d}S_{xa}(S^{-T})_{vd}[a=d][y=u]
=(SS^{-1})_{xv}[y=u]
=[x=v][y=u].
\tag{1}
\]

The same construction applies independently to \(Y\) and \(U\).
Constant maps do not increase the polynomial degree. An invertible
constant four-leg transformation also preserves the ideal generated by
all tensor entries, so it cannot introduce a new common entry factor.

The implemented elementary matrix is

\[
S=I+aE_{ij},\qquad i\ne j,\qquad
a\in\mathbf F_4^\times.
\tag{2}
\]

Since \(E_{ij}^2=0\) and the characteristic is two, \(S^{-1}=S\).
Thus (2) adds \(a\) times physical row \(j\) to row \(i\) on the
first label, and adds \(a\) times row \(i\) to row \(j\) on its
matching label. Formula (1) proves endpoint preservation for every
word, including noncommuting words on the same label pair.

The two weighted cuts are recomputed from the transformed polynomial
tensor. A constant physical matrix need not be regular after conjugation
by a nonconstant weight matrix. Therefore the search does not treat
weighted profiles as invariant under these moves.

## 2. The completed finite schedule

There are twenty ordered pairs \((i,j)\) of distinct five-letter labels.
For each of the two paired boundaries, the single-move scan uses every
ordered pair and coefficients \(1,\omega\), where
\(\omega^2=\omega+1\). This gives
\(2\cdot20\cdot2=80\) queries.

The reference tensor is binary. Coefficient Frobenius fixes it and
preserves every determinantal valuation. The two nonbinary coefficients
are conjugate, so the single-move list represents all nonzero
coefficients. For a pair of moves the coefficient representatives are

\[
(1,1),\ (1,\omega),\ (\omega,1),\
(\omega,\omega),\ (\omega,\omega^2).
\tag{3}
\]

The pair schedule chooses one move on \(X/V\) and one on \(Y/U\).
Those operations commute because they act on different physical labels.
Every choice of ordered label pairs and every orbit in (3) is tested,
giving \(20^2\cdot5=2,000\) queries. This is a finite subset of the
paired general linear group; it is not an enumeration of that group.

Each tensor is evaluated at the paired energies
\((0,10,6,2,8)\) and \((0,12,6,0,8)\). The local heights are one-half
of these energies on \(U,V\) at infinity and on \(X,Y\) at zero.
Both complete 25-by-25 profiles are used in the Gibbs-root objective.

The [complete search record](../evidence/paired-physical-transvection-search.json)
stores all 2,080 operation lists, tensor digests, profiles, and numerical
costs. Including the unchanged reference, it contains 244 distinct
profile pairs at the natural gauge and 221 at the two-zero gauge.
All 1,300,625 endpoint entries from the reference and queried tensors
were checked directly. The best nonidentity move at the natural gauge
has recorded cost about `0.7898206076`, above the reference.

## 3. Independent exact verification

The [control record](../evidence/paired-physical-transvection-controls.json)
uses eight operation chains of lengths
\(1,1,2,3,5,8,12,16\). It reconstructs four complete physical matrices,
checks their paired inverse identities, and contracts all four maps
directly with the source tensor. This does not use the production
sequential tensor-update routine.

The controls verify 5,000 literal coefficient identities, 5,000
Frobenius coefficient identities, 400 paired inverse matrix entries,
and sixteen complete profiles through exact regular polynomial frames.
They include noncommuting words and genuine nonbinary coefficients.

The [saved-search replay](../evidence/paired-physical-transvection-search-replay.json)
independently reconstructs every retained tensor by the same four-map
contraction and certifies its complete profiles through regular
polynomial frames. Its 48 retained witnesses require 32 distinct
four-map contractions and 100 complete profile certificates, including
the two references. It checks 31,250 endpoint entries across gauges
and separately recomputes numerical costs. The
profile certificates do not call the native Smith oracle used in the
search.

## 4. Reproduction

The exact transformation is in
[`paired_physical_transvections.py`](../tools/paired_physical_transvections.py).
The finite driver records its source hashes before searching.

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 tools/check_paired_physical_transvections.py \
  --output /tmp/paired-physical-transvection-controls.json
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 tools/search_paired_physical_transvections.py \
  --seconds 1800 --output /tmp/paired-physical-transvection-search.json
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 tools/check_paired_physical_transvections.py \
  --search evidence/paired-physical-transvection-search.json \
  --output /tmp/paired-physical-transvection-search-replay.json
```

The time budget is a stopping condition. The retained run completed
the entire requested schedule; a shorter prefix must retain its
incomplete status. No favorable numerical pressure is promoted to a
factoring improvement without an independent full-profile replay and
a rigorous inequality certificate.
