# Targeted nine-term corrections from the first rank-nine image

The first explicit rank-nine vector in the two-copy low image flag
is the tensor square of the one-copy rank-three image at relative
order three. This note gives its sparse physical factors, exact
degree windows for endpoint-zero corrections, and a leading-jet
obstruction for matched nine-term couplings.

These templates are concrete search inputs. Their leading infinity
coefficient necessarily adds at least seven channels at relative
order six. That statement does not exclude a compensating change
elsewhere in the complete profiles, and is not a global pressure
floor for the template family.

## 1. Sparse physical factors

Use the retained source's label order and the standard five-letter
coordinate vectors. Its third constant image vector, reshaped as
a five-by-five matrix, is

\[
 S_2=(e_2+e_3)e_0^T+(e_0+e_4)e_2^T
                         +(e_1+e_2+e_3)e_4^T.          \tag{1}
\]

Its binary encoding is `4769284`. Write the three left factors
as `A_0,A_1,A_2` and their coordinate indices as
`k=(0,2,4)`. For an ordered pair `I=(i,j)`, put

\[
 L_I=A_i\otimes A_j,\qquad E_I=e_{k_i}\otimes e_{k_j}.
\]

The nine matrices `L_I E_I^T` are independent, and their sum is
`S_2 tensor S_2`. Thus that rank-nine image is expressed using
nine physically rank-one matrices on the 25-letter boundaries.

For a permutation `pi` of the nine pairs, the four-leg matched
correction has the form

\[
 \Delta(z)=\sum_I c_I\lambda_I(z)
       L_I(X)L_{\pi(I)}(Y)E_I(U)E_{\pi(I)}(V),           \tag{2}
\]

where every `c_I` is nonzero and every `lambda_I(1)=0`.
It is a sum of nine pure terms. It is different from the complete
outer product of the rank-nine image and coimage, which would
have 81 such products.

## 2. Exact flag intersections

Let `F` be the span of the nine physical image matrices in (1)
after taking their tensor products. Let `U_b` be the reference
two-copy constant image flag through relative order `b`.
The exact intersections are

\[
 \dim(F\cap U_b)=
 \begin{cases}
 0,&0\le b\le5,\\
 1,&6\le b\le9.
 \end{cases}                                           \tag{3}
\]

For `6<=b<=9`, the intersection is generated by the sum of all
nine factors. Its coefficient vector is `(1,...,1)`, so no proper
subset of the factors spans a nonzero vector in this intersection.
The coimage factors satisfy the same statements. These are
dimensions of binary matrices and therefore persist under every
characteristic-two extension of the coefficient field.

## 3. A leading-jet obstruction for every pairing and weighting

Suppose every term in (2) first appears at relative infinity order
six. Earlier local coefficients are unchanged. The reference has
fourteen channels of relative order less than six and five channels
of order exactly six.

After eliminating the earlier channels, let `A` be the order-six
coefficient and `H` the new matched coefficient. Then

\[
 \operatorname{rank}A=5,\qquad \operatorname{rank}H=9.
\]

The second equality holds for every pairing and nonzero weighting:
the two nine-element factor families are independent and the
coupling between them is an invertible monomial matrix.
By (3), the image intersections and row-space intersections of
`A,H` each have dimension one.

For any two matrices,

\[
 \operatorname{rank}(A+H)\ge
 \operatorname{rank}A+\operatorname{rank}H
 -\dim(\operatorname{im}A\cap\operatorname{im}H)
 -\dim(\operatorname{row}A\cap\operatorname{row}H).        \tag{4}
\]

Indeed, the stacked map `v -> (Av,Hv)` has rank equal to the
dimension of the sum of the two row spaces. The addition map on
the direct sum of the two images has kernel dimension equal to
their image intersection. Composing the maps proves (4).
It follows here that

\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{rank}(A+H)\ge5+9-1-1=12.}          \tag{5}
\]

Thus the matched correction adds at least seven order-six channels.
A proper nonempty subset of the terms has zero intersection with
the reference quotient on both sides and adds every participating
channel. These conclusions hold for all pairings and all nonzero
weights over any characteristic-two coefficient field.

Regular-frame higher coefficients cannot alter this leading-jet
calculation. Eliminating an earlier channel of order at most five
can only create a quadratic correction of order at least
`2*6-5=7`. Equation (5) concerns the exact order-six coefficient.

It does not imply a lower bound on the complete pressure. Later
orders can change, and the zero-place profile is separate.

## 4. Degree windows and the balanced pairing

The natural one-copy half-heights are

\[
 h=(0,5,3,1,4).
\]

For each pair `I`, define

\[
 a_I=\min h(\operatorname{supp}L_I),\qquad
 b_I=h(k_i)+h(k_j),\qquad \sigma_I=a_I+b_I.
\]

Here the first minimum uses the sum of the two copy heights.
In lexicographic pair order, the three arrays are

```
a     = (2,1,2,1,0,1,2,1,2)
b     = (0,3,4,3,6,7,4,7,8)
sigma = (2,4,6,4,6,8,6,8,10)
```

The reference infinity minimum is `-16`. Aligning (2) at relative
order six therefore means absolute order `-10`. The required top
degree of term `I` is

\[
 d_I=10+b_I+b_{\pi(I)}.                                \tag{6}
\]

If its lowest polynomial degree is `ell_I`, its minimum zero-place
entry order is

\[
 j_I=\ell_I+a_I+a_{\pi(I)}.                            \tag{7}
\]

Two useful endpoint-zero polynomials with these extremes are

\[
 z^{\ell_I}+z^{d_I},\qquad
 z^{\ell_I}(1+z)^{d_I-\ell_I},\qquad \ell_I<d_I.
\]

The antitone pairing
`pi(i,j)=(2-i,2-j)` has permutation list
`(8,7,6,5,4,3,2,1,0)`. It gives
`sigma_I+sigma_pi(I)=12` for every term. Therefore each term may
have any minimum zero-place entry order from 16 through 21 while
retaining the infinity alignment and the complete degree bound 36.

No pairing can make every zero-place first order exceed 21 within
these windows. Since `sum sigma_I=54`, the matched sums have average
twelve. At least one is at most twelve, and the endpoint-zero
condition forces `ell_I<=d_I-1`. Equations (6) and (7) then give
`j_I<=21` for that term. The antitone pairing attains this bound
simultaneously for all nine terms. Their `U` coordinates are
distinct, so the raw entry supports of different terms do not
cancel this conclusion.
