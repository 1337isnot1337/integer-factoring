# Deterministic two-part extraction from unsaturated relations

## Result and scope

Let G be a finite abelian group given by canonical hashable identifiers and
deterministic multiplication/inversion. Suppose m supplied elements generate
G, a nonzero integer E annihilates all of them, and log2|G|<=B. There is a
deterministic algorithm that extracts a cyclic basis of the Sylow two-subgroup
using

```math
O\!\left(m\,\operatorname{bits}(E)+
mB\,[rB+2^{\lceil r/2\rceil}]\right)
```

group operations, where r=dim_F2 G[2]. It uses no factorization of E, no
exact class number, no random group elements, and no saturation of a relation
lattice. These are generic-group operation counts, to be multiplied by the
bit cost of canonical group operations in an application.

The implementation is [arithmetic_two_group.py](../tools/arithmetic_two_group.py).
The argument is a simple prime-power basis algorithm in the tradition of
[Sutherland's structure/discrete-log work](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.3413);
the proof below is self-contained. No novelty claim is made for generic
abelian group algorithms. Its role here is to replace the random torsion
coverage step in an arithmetic factoring framework.

## 1. A relation determinant supplies the annihilator

Let g_1,...,g_m generate G. Suppose an m-by-m integer matrix A has nonzero
determinant and each row is a relation among these generators. The adjugate
identity implies that det(A)e_i lies in the integer row lattice for every i.
Thus E=|det A| annihilates every generator and the whole group.

Write E=2^s u with u odd. Then h_i=g_i^u generate the Sylow two-subgroup:
u kills the odd-order part and is an automorphism of the two-part. Computing
u only requires stripping powers of two, not factoring the odd number u.

The relation lattice may be much smaller than the complete kernel. In
particular, direct halving of its even relations may miss most of G[2].
The determinant projection followed by the basis algorithm avoids that issue.

## 2. Cyclic generators with independent order-two tops

Maintain elements b_j of orders 2^k_j such that
t_j=b_j^(2^(k_j-1)) are linearly independent in G[2]. These elements give
a direct product of cyclic subgroups: any nonzero relation among the b_j,
after multiplying by a suitable power of two, would give a nonzero binary
relation among their tops.

For a new candidate h of order 2^k, compute its top t. Meet-in-the-middle
subset products determine whether t lies in the span of the existing t_j,
using O(2^ceil(r/2)) group operations and stored identifiers.

If it does not, append h to the basis. If it does, write
t=product_(j in J) t_j.

- If k<=k_j for every j in J, multiply h by the inverses of
  b_j^(2^(k_j-k)). Its top cancels, so its order strictly decreases.
- Otherwise choose j in J with smallest k_j and replace that basis element
  by h. The replaced element becomes the candidate. The same binary
  relation expresses its top using the updated basis, whose relevant orders
  are now all at least k_j; cancel that top as above.

Replacing one top by a binary combination containing it preserves
independence. Both operations preserve the subgroup generated by the basis
and candidate. Each reduction strictly lowers the candidate's order, even
after a swap, so at most B reductions occur per input. At termination the
basis generates all supplied elements and has independent tops. Those tops
are a basis of the entire G[2], not just the tops of the original inputs.

The bound B limits actual element order. It need not equal the two-adic
valuation of E, which can be much larger when the supplied annihilator has
unnecessary powers of two.

## 3. Class-group consequence after removing small factors

For a negative discriminant Delta, reduced binary quadratic forms give
canonical class identifiers and deterministic polynomial-bit-cost group
operations. Lenstra--Pomerance Section 2, especially Theorem 2.5, identifies
ambiguous forms with G[2] and maps them onto coprime factorizations of the
odd part of Delta. That map is a group homomorphism in the stated
factorization group.

If Delta=-dN with a bounded multiplier d coprime to N, projection onto the
prime-power factors of N remains a homomorphism. For N with at least two
distinct prime factors, its image is nontrivial. Therefore some element of
a basis of G[2] gives a proper factor of N. The ambiguous-form formulas and
a gcd find it; randomness in the torsion output is unnecessary once the
whole basis is available. Prime powers are handled separately.

Trial division through 2^ceil(sqrt(b)), where b=bits(N), either finds a
factor or leaves at most sqrt(b)+O(1) distinct prime factors. Genus theory
then bounds r by sqrt(b)+O(1). Thus the exponential part of the generic
basis extraction is only 2^O(sqrt(b)). If m and bits(E) have the same bound,
the whole extraction fits that scale. This observation does not construct
the class-group generators or relation matrix.

## 4. Exact controls

Run `python3 tools/check_arithmetic_two_group.py`. The
[record](../evidence/arithmetic-two-group.json) checks 703 product groups,
including groups whose complete order is over four trillion. The routine
uses only their black-box operations; the checker independently knows their
coordinates and validates the output orders and all order-two directions.
The cases include odd components and deliberately oversaturated annihilators.

The important mixed-generator control is Z/8 x Z/2 with generators
(1,0),(1,1). Their individual final powers coincide, so simply taking those
powers loses a direction. The algorithm instead finds cyclic orders 8 and 2.
The order-swap control presents a short generator before a longer dependent
top and exercises the replacement step.

The [positive-kernel pipeline](positive-relation-kernel.md) also constructs
an unsaturated relation matrix of determinant 5,120 for a group of order 16.
Halving the displayed relation rows yields only two group elements; the
determinant projection and generic basis algorithm recover all sixteen.

These are exact finite controls and an ordinary generic-group proof. No new
factoring runtime follows without the arithmetic generator and relation
construction obligations. This work used substantial OpenAI Codex assistance
and adds no Lean formalization.
