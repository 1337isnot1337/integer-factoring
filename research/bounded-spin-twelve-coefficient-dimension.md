# The twelve-site coefficient algebra has dimension ten

## Result

The twelve-site bounded-spin coefficient span is exactly ten-dimensional.
The upper bound is the uniform `n-2` coefficient identity; the lower
bound is an explicit rank-ten minor.

This corrects a natural but false guess that the twelve-site algebra
would remain eight-dimensional like the published ten-site model. The
ten-site `8 x 8` regular representation and its seven idempotents cannot
be transplanted to twelve sites. The next object is a ten-dimensional
twelve-site multiplication table, not a larger instance of the ten-site
one.

No primary decomposition, cut-rank bound, or factoring score changes.

## Upper bound

[The three uniform jet identities](bounded-spin-three-jets.md) give, for
even `n >= 6`,

```math
2C_1+nC_0=0,\qquad
2\sum_{j=0}^n jC_j-n\sum_{j=0}^n C_j=0,\qquad
2C_{n-1}+nC_n=0.
```

The three constraints are independent over every odd characteristic.
Hence the coefficient span has dimension at most `n-2`. At `n=12` this
is `10`.

## Lower bound

Evaluate the normalized original-pencil coefficient vector on ten
explicit path quadruples. The checker records the quadruples and verifies
that the resulting `10 x 13` matrix has rank ten; its `10 x 10` minor is

```text
coefficient indices: 0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10.
```

The same evaluation at ten sites reproduces the published eight-dimensional
basis

```text
0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10,
```

which is an independent layout control: the constructor and coefficient
ordering recover the known ten-site model before the twelve-site minor is
read.

Thus the twelve-site dimension is at least ten and at most ten, hence
exactly ten.

## The `R,T` subalgebra is at least eight-dimensional

The [coarse transposition spectrum](bounded-spin-coarse-twelve.md) has six
joint generalized eigenspaces, but those blocks do not exhaust the algebra
generated by the replica swap `R` and the class sum `T`. On the
`89^2=7921`-state module, the orbit of a deterministic generic vector under
`R,T` has dimension `8` for five independent seeds; the ten-site control
has orbit dimension `6`, matching the known six-dimensional ten-site
subalgebra. Hence

```text
dim <R,T>_{12} >= 8,    dim <R,T>_{10} = 6.
```

The full twelve-site coefficient algebra has dimension `10`, so at most
two further algebra directions can remain. The coarse six-block partition
is therefore not the full `R,T` algebra: there is at least one additional
generalized direction. On the orbit, the actions of `R,T` are explicit
`8 x 8` matrices; the generated matrix algebra has dimension `8`, and its
six joint generalized blocks have dimensions

```text
(0,+):1  (0,-):1  (1,+):1  (1,-):1  (4,+):2  (4,-):2.
```

This is a finite-dimensional model of the `R,T` part only. The exact
subalgebra dimension, the two missing coefficient directions, and the full
primary refinement remain open.

**Update.** The coefficient-algebra image is now exact: `R` is the table
unit `C_0` and the class sum `T` generates a five-dimensional subalgebra,
so `dim <R,T> = 5` in the regular representation. The orbit dimension `8`
above is a statement about the action on the `7921`-state module, not about
the coefficient algebra. The ten-site completing basis direction `b_4`
only reaches `7` at twelve sites, while the ten-site separator sum
`b_2+b_4` completes the algebra. See
[the separator-direction note](bounded-spin-twelve-missing-direction.md).

## What is now reduced

The twelve-site primary calculation needs:

1. the ten-dimensional coefficient multiplication table or regular
   representation at length twelve, using the eight-dimensional `R,T`
   orbit model as a starting point;
2. the central idempotents in that ten-dimensional algebra;
3. the exact rank of each idempotent, or an exact trace form that
   determines it.

The dimension is no longer an unknown. The ten-site idempotents and
their seven primary dimensions are not a valid short cut, because the
algebra grows by two dimensions.

## Reproduction

```sh
python3 tools/check_bounded_spin_twelve_coefficient_dimension.py
make verify-bounded-spin-twelve-coefficient-dimension
python3 tools/check_bounded_spin_twelve_RT_orbit.py
make verify-bounded-spin-twelve-RT-orbit
```

The dimension checker samples 50,000 quadruples to find the witness, then
verifies the stored ten-row minor exactly. The orbit checker builds the
twelve-site frame and five generic orbits; it writes
`evidence/bounded-spin-twelve-RT-orbit.json`.
