# Dense smooth forms give a global class-number multiple

Full class-group generators are not required for the **class-number-multiple**
stage. A dense smooth subset and a full-rank relation matrix on the small
factor base suffice. This removes a stronger generation requirement from
that stage, conditional on efficient arithmetic pair extraction. Turning
the multiple into a deterministic split still needs enough ambiguous
directions; density alone does not supply them.

## An elementary index transfer

Let H be a subgroup of a finite abelian group G. Suppose S is a subset of
H with |S|>=delta*|G|, for a known positive lower bound delta. Then
[G:H]<=1/delta. Take a certified integer Q>=1/delta and put

```math
M_Q=\operatorname{lcm}(1,2,\ldots,Q).
```

If E_H annihilates H, then E_H*M_Q annihilates G. The quotient G/H has
order d=[G:H]<=Q, so g^d lies in H for every g, and d divides M_Q.

If E_H is the absolute determinant of a full-rank relation matrix on
generators of H, then |H| divides E_H. The row lattice is contained in the
kernel of the surjection Z^m -> H, so its index is a multiple of |H|.
Consequently |G|=|H|d divides E_H*M_Q. Thus we get a **class-number
multiple**, not merely an exponent multiple, without proving H=G. An
arbitrary annihilator of H alone gives only the weaker conclusion.

The correction has O(Q log(Q+1)) bits by the factorial bound. Repeated
gcd/lcm computes it at polynomial bit cost in Q. If
Q=exp(O(sqrt(b log b))), this stays at the same scale with a different
constant. The numerical value of M_Q is not its encoding length.

## Application to the positive relation kernel

For G=C_Delta, let H be generated by prime forms at primes <=Y coprime
to Delta. They generate H by definition, even when generation of G is
unknown. Enumerating these primes and testing roots modulo 4p constructs
them deterministically at polynomial cost in Y and log|Delta|. A simple
brute-force construction suffices at subexponential Y; no fast randomized
modular root algorithm is being assumed.

Every reduced form with Y-smooth leading coefficient coprime to Delta lies
in H, with a known short prime-form word (Lenstra--Pomerance Section 2.8).
Suppose an applicable density theorem gives |S|>=delta*|G|. Its density
within H is at least delta. The [biased-word theorem](arithmetic-biased-words.md)
and [positive kernel](positive-relation-kernel.md) therefore apply to H
using only these deterministically constructed generators.

An efficient smooth-pair extractor for the required targets would give a
diagonally dominant full-rank relation matrix on H. Its determinant and the
index correction give a global class-number multiple. This route does not
enumerate the large prime interval used for full-group generation in
Lenstra--Pomerance. The extractor remains unproved; Fourier existence is
not an extraction algorithm.

The original sufficient contract, with full generators and relations, still
implies complete two-torsion recovery. This alternative contract reaches a
global class-number multiple from small-prime data, with a separate
deterministic splitting/coverage obligation.

## Why the last obligation is real

For Delta=-348=-4*87 and Y=7, there are six reduced classes. The three
smooth classes are (1,0,87), (7,-4,13) and (7,4,13). The factor-base
subgroup H has order three, so delta=1/2 and Q=2 suffice. Its one
nontrivial prime-form generator has order three: E_H=3. The correction
M_2=2 gives global class-number multiple 6.

But H has trivial two-torsion. Two-part extraction from its generators
returns no nonidentity ambiguous form, even when given the global multiple.
The full class group has two ambiguous classes; the missing form (3,0,29)
supplies a factor of 87. A global annihilator does not create missing directions.
This control forbids silently promoting the transfer to a factoring bound.

## Evidence

Run `python3 tools/check_arithmetic_density_lift.py`. The
[record](../evidence/arithmetic-density-lift.json) checks all 256 negative
discriminants with absolute value between 3 and 512 that are 0 or 1 modulo
4. In 54 controls the factor base generates a proper subgroup; in 41 its
diagonal relation determinant fails to annihilate the full group before
correction. Every corrected value is a multiple of the independently
enumerated class number and annihilates every class. In 51 controls the
subgroup misses some global two-torsion.

Class enumeration and order search are only finite verification tools.
The general transfer is the elementary proof above. Neither these controls
nor that transfer supply fast pair extraction or a new factoring score.
