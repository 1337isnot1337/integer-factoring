# One evaluation generates the entire joint energy algebra

For the five-letter reference, every evaluation `A(a)` away from `a=0,1`,
together with the integer energy projections, generates the same
169-dimensional algebra as all coefficients of `A(t)`. This holds over
**every** characteristic-two field. The proof exhibits a polynomial
triangular minor equal to `t^1452(t+1)^350`.

Consequently the graded invariant-subspace lattice is independent of
the evaluation point away from zero and one. More strongly, every
rational energy-preserving **matrix** gauge regular and invertible at
zero, one and infinity that produces a polynomial seed reduces to a
constant conjugation. A gauge equal to the identity at infinity leaves
the tensor unchanged. This is a global theorem, without a formal-family
or single-projector assumption. It does not improve the complete
factoring score `1/2`.

## 1. The constant coefficient algebra

Use `A(t)=T(t)S` and the 25 joint states of
[the reference](joint-energy-conjugations.md). Write

```math
A(t)=\sum_{j=0}^{18}t^j A_j.
```

Let `E_h` project onto the exact integer joint-energy block `h`. There
are eleven blocks. Define the unital binary matrix algebra

```math
\mathscr A=\operatorname{alg}_{\mathbf F_2}
                  (E_h,A_0,\ldots,A_{18}).             \tag{1}
```

Exact closure under left multiplication by the generators gives
`dim A=169`. Starting with all `E_h` suffices: their sum is the identity,
and arbitrary products are words built by successive left multiplications.
Binary independence persists over every characteristic-two extension.

For a field `K` and `a in K`, let

```math
\mathscr A(a)=\operatorname{alg}_{K}(E_h,A(a)).
```

Every generator belongs to `A tensor K`, so `A(a)` is always a subalgebra
of that constant algebra. The substantive direction is the reverse
inclusion at every good point.

## 2. A polynomial certificate valid at all good points

Work first over `R=F2[t]`. Form the `R`-algebra generated by the `E_h`
and the single polynomial matrix `A(t)`. Its elements include every
energy block `E_h A(t) E_k` and every product of such blocks.

The checker constructs an echelon basis of its underlying polynomial
row module, viewing each matrix as a vector of its 625 entries. It keeps
separate modules for each `(row energy,column energy)` block. Each block
has at most sixteen entries. The operations are:

1. Start with the eleven energy projections.
2. Left-multiply current members by each compatible block of `A(t)`.
3. Insert the resulting vector using polynomial Euclidean elimination.
   Subtract polynomial multiples of earlier rows; when a nonzero pivot
   remainder has smaller degree, exchange the two rows and continue.
4. Process every new or improved row in a first-in, first-out queue.

Every operation preserves membership in the generated polynomial
algebra: all coefficients and Euclidean quotients are in `F2[t]`.
No rational pivot is inverted. The finite replay processes 632 members
and ends with 169 basis vectors. A separate pass verifies membership
of all 942 compatible left-generator products in the terminal module.

For each terminal row, the last nonzero entry is its pivot. The pivot
positions are all distinct, including across energy blocks. Exact
division shows that every pivot is

```math
t^{e_i}(t+1)^{f_i},\qquad e_i,f_i\ge0,
\qquad \sum_i e_i=1452,\quad\sum_i f_i=350.             \tag{2}
```

Select those 169 pivot columns and order rows and columns by pivot.
The selected polynomial matrix is triangular, so its determinant is

```math
\boxed{t^{1452}(t+1)^{350}.}                           \tag{3}
```

The [certificate](../evidence/joint-evaluation-algebra.json) retains all
169 polynomial vectors, their pivots and exponents. The checker also
verifies that all 3,620 nonzero coefficient matrices appearing in these
vectors belong to the constant algebra (1).

Now evaluate at any `a` in any characteristic-two field with `a!=0,1`.
Equation (3) is nonzero, so the evaluated vectors are independent.
Each evaluated vector is a polynomial combination of words in
`A(a)` and the energy projections. Thus

```math
169\le\dim_K\mathscr A(a)
\le\dim_K(\mathscr A\otimes K)=169,
```

which proves

```math
\boxed{\mathscr A(a)=\mathscr A\otimes K
                       \quad(a\ne0,1).}              \tag{4}
```

At the excluded points the dimensions are exactly 19 at zero and 11
at one. These binary dimensions also persist under field extension.
As independent literal controls, direct algebra generation over `F4`
gives dimensions `19,11,169,169` at its four points. Those finite
controls do not replace the all-field polynomial argument (2)--(4).

## 3. Invariant subspaces and constant orbit identification

Call a subspace graded if it is invariant under every `E_h`.
For any such subspace `W` and any `a!=0,1`, equation (4) gives

```math
A(a)W\subseteq W
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A_jW\subseteq W
                         \text{ for every }j.         \tag{5}
```

In particular the full graded invariant-subspace lattice of the
polynomial family can be recovered from a single good evaluation.
Likewise an energy-preserving constant matrix commuting with one
`A(a)` commutes with every coefficient. An invertible such matrix
therefore leaves the whole seed unchanged. Two constant energy
conjugates agreeing at one good point agree identically as polynomial
tensors. These statements concern exact algebra, not numerical
reconstruction from approximate matrix values.

## 4. Global rational energy gauges regular at three places

The same polynomial certificate proves the localization identity

```math
\operatorname{alg}_{S}(E_h,A(t))=\mathscr A\otimes S,
\qquad S=K[t,t^{-1},(t+1)^{-1}].
```

To see this, restrict the constant algebra to the 169 selected pivot
columns. That binary coordinate map has full rank 169, also checked
directly. The polynomial basis has determinant (3) in those coordinates,
which is a unit in `S`. Its inverse therefore has entries in `S`, and
every constant member of `A` is an `S`-linear combination of the
polynomial algebra members. The reverse inclusion was already clear.

**Global theorem.** Let `U(t)` be any invertible rational energy-block
matrix over `K(t)`. Assume that both `U` and its inverse are regular
at `t=0`, `t=1` and infinity, and that `A_U=UAU^-1` is polynomial.
Then

```math
U(t)=U(0)W(t),\qquad [W(t),X]=0\quad(X\in\mathscr A),
\qquad A_U=U(0)A(t)U(0)^{-1}.
```

In particular if `U(infinity)=I`, then `A_U=A`. There is no generator,
idempotence, nilpotency, leading-rank, or identity-component hypothesis.

For any constant `X in A`, use the localization identity to express
`X` as an `S`-linear combination of words in `A(t)` and the `E_h`.
Energy preservation gives `UE_hU^-1=E_h`. Conjugating that expression
shows that

```math
U(t)XU(t)^{-1}\in\operatorname{Mat}_{25}(S),
```

because the substituted `A_U` is polynomial. Thus these rational
entries have no finite poles except possibly at zero and one. The
assumed regularity of `U,U^-1` removes those two possibilities.
Their regularity at infinity removes a pole there as well. A rational
function with no poles on the projective line is constant; equivalently
the absence of finite poles makes it a polynomial, and regularity at
infinity makes its degree zero. This argument is valid over arbitrary
`K`, without requiring its algebraic closure.

Evaluating at zero proves
`UXU^-1=U(0)XU(0)^-1` for every `X`. This is precisely the stated
factorization with a full-commutant rational unit. Evaluating at
infinity instead proves the normalized conclusion when `U(infinity)=I`.
The full graded commutant is the four-dimensional algebra from the
predecessor note, now extended to the field `K(t)`.

## 5. Scalar-projector corollary and its invariant-subspace proof

**Corollary.** Let `K` be any characteristic-two field and let `P` be any
constant energy-block idempotent. Put `Q=I-P`. Let `f(t) in K(t)` be
nonconstant and have nonzero finite values at `0`, `1` and infinity.
If

```math
U(t)=f(t)P+Q,\qquad A_U=UAU^{-1}                       \tag{6}
```

has polynomial entries, then `P` is one of the four neutral projectors
from the [projector classification](joint-monomial-lattice-modifications.md#43-the-terminal-space-has-exactly-four-idempotents),
and `A_U=A`. No leading-rank condition is required in this theorem.

To prove it, extend the coefficient field to an algebraic closure.
A nonconstant rational function regular and nonzero at infinity has
both a finite zero `a` and a finite pole `b`. Regularity and nonvanishing
at zero and one ensure `a,b!=0,1`. They are distinct.

Projecting the conjugation onto its two off-diagonal blocks gives

```math
QA_UP=f^{-1}QAP,\qquad PA_UQ=fPAQ.                     \tag{7}
```

Polynomiality at the zero and pole respectively forces

```math
QA(a)P=0,\qquad PA(b)Q=0.                             \tag{8}
```

Thus `im P` is graded and invariant under `A(a)`, while `ker P=im Q`
is graded and invariant under `A(b)`. Equation (5) makes both subspaces
invariant under every `A_j`. The idempotent splitting is therefore a
splitting into invariant subspaces, so `[P,A_j]=0` for every `j`.
This equality descends to the original field. Equation (6) is trivial.

For completeness, the full graded commutant has dimension four. The
checker verifies that it is commutative, with a two-dimensional
square-zero radical and complementary coordinate idempotents `E,F`
of ranks 13 and 12. Every element is uniquely
`alpha E+beta F+R`, where `R` lies in the radical. Its square is
`alpha^2 E+beta^2 F`. Idempotence therefore forces `R=0` and
`alpha,beta in {0,1}` over any field in scope. This proves the exact
four-projector conclusion without enumerating field elements.

The theorem includes every `f(t)=(t-a)/(t-b)` regular and invertible at
the three distinguished places, arbitrary higher multiplicities,
irreducible numerator and denominator factors, and nonnormalized
nonzero constant value at infinity. Passing to algebraic roots in the
proof makes no algorithmic assumption about finding those roots.
