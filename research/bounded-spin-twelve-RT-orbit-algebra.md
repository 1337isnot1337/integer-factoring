# The twelve-site `R,T` orbit algebra is `F5^4 x dual x dual`

## Result

The algebra generated by the replica swap `R` and the transposition class
sum `T` on a generic twelve-site orbit is exactly

```math
\langle R,T\rangle_{12}\cong \mathbb F_5^{\,4}\times
  \mathbb F_5[\varepsilon_1]/(\varepsilon_1^2)\times
  \mathbb F_5[\varepsilon_2]/(\varepsilon_2^2),
```

of dimension `8`. It has six primitive central idempotents, four scalar
blocks and two dual blocks, with regular ranks `1,1,1,1,2,2` (sum `8`).
Its trace form has rank `6` and a two-dimensional square-zero radical.

The [full coefficient algebra](bounded-spin-twelve-coefficient-idempotents.md)
is the larger

```math
A_{12}\cong \mathbb F_5^{\,6}\times
  \mathbb F_5[\varepsilon_1]/(\varepsilon_1^2)\times
  \mathbb F_5[\varepsilon_2]/(\varepsilon_2^2),
```

of dimension `10`, with regular ranks `1,1,1,1,1,1,2,2`. Thus the orbit
model has the abstract type of a quotient of `A_{12}` by an ideal spanned
by two scalar blocks. The two dual blocks are both visible; the four
scalar orbit blocks are four of the six scalar coefficient blocks.

This is an **abstract structure theorem for the orbit algebra**, not an
identification of an actual map `A_{12} -> <R,T>`. Which two scalar
coefficient blocks are invisible to `R,T`, and how the orbit sits inside
the cut module, remain open.

## Method

The [orbit certificate](../evidence/bounded-spin-twelve-RT-orbit.json)
stores the `8 x 8` actions of `R` and `T` on a generic cyclic orbit, whose
generated matrix algebra has dimension `8`. The
[builder](../tools/build_bounded_spin_twelve_RT_orbit_algebra.py) closes
those two matrices under multiplication, solves for the multiplication
table in the resulting matrix basis, and checks commutativity,
associativity, and unitality. It then applies the same minimal-polynomial
and CRT decomposition used for the coefficient algebra.

The decomposition returns six primitive idempotents. Each block is checked
independently:

- four scalar blocks have trace form `[[1]]`;
- two dual blocks have rank-one trace form and an explicit nonzero
  nilpotent `\varepsilon` with `\varepsilon^2=0` and
  `e\varepsilon=\varepsilon`;
- the two nilpotents span the two-dimensional radical and are
  square-zero.

The six orbit blocks match the six joint generalized eigenspaces of `R,T`
recorded in the orbit certificate: four one-dimensional blocks and two
two-dimensional blocks. The algebra generated on the orbit is therefore
the direct sum of those six joint blocks.

## What changed

The [dimension note](bounded-spin-twelve-coefficient-dimension.md) proved
`dim <R,T>_{12} >= 8` and left the exact subalgebra and the two missing
coefficient directions open. The orbit algebra is now identified
abstractly. The two extra coefficient dimensions beyond the orbit are
**scalar**, not additional dual directions: the coefficient algebra has two
more scalar blocks than the orbit algebra.

## What does not follow

- No embedding of the orbit algebra into `A_{12}` is computed, so the two
  scalar coefficient blocks invisible to `R,T` are not named.
- No cut-module rank, two-cut pairing, or rank partition is computed.
- The complete factoring bound remains `1/2`; no crossing is claimed.
